# Shipping & Transport Policy

## Transport Mode Selection
- Air: used for shipments under 500kg requiring transit time under 5
  days, or for high-value goods (unit cost above $200).
- Sea: default for shipments over 1000km where transit time over 20
  days is acceptable; lowest cost per kg.
- Road: used for intra-region shipments under 1500km.
- Rail: used where available as a lower-emission alternative to road
  for shipments between 500km and 3000km.

## Warehouse Congestion
A warehouse is considered congested when active shipments exceed 80%
of its stated capacity. Orders routed through a congested warehouse
should expect additional handling delay of 1-3 days.

## Incident Escalation
- CUSTOMS_DELAY, DOCUMENTATION: handled by the trade compliance team.
- WEATHER, MECHANICAL: handled by the carrier relationship team.
- DAMAGE, LOST: escalated to the Supplier under Section 4 of the
  Supplier Contract and logged for insurance claims if value exceeds
  $1000.

## Review Cadence
Supplier delay rates and incident counts are reviewed monthly. Any
supplier crossing the 25% delay rate threshold (see Supplier Contract,
Section 3) is flagged to procurement automatically.
