# Supplier Contract — Standard Terms (Template)

## Section 1 — Delivery Obligations
The Supplier shall deliver goods within the agreed transit window
stated on each purchase order. A delivery is considered late if the
actual delivery date exceeds the expected delivery date stated at
shipment creation.

## Section 2 — Late Delivery Penalties
If a Supplier delivers more than five (5) days late on a given
shipment, the Buyer may apply a penalty of 2% of the order value per
additional day of delay, capped at 20% of the order value. Penalties
do not apply if the delay is caused by customs clearance, severe
weather, or other events outside the Supplier's reasonable control.

## Section 3 — Repeated Delays
If a Supplier's delay rate (share of shipments delivered late) exceeds
25% over a rolling 90-day period, the Buyer may place the Supplier on
a formal improvement plan. If the delay rate remains above 25% after
the improvement plan period, the Buyer may terminate the contract with
30 days' written notice.

## Section 4 — Damaged Goods
Goods arriving damaged must be reported within 48 hours of delivery.
The Supplier is liable for replacement or refund of damaged goods
unless damage is demonstrably caused by the carrier during transport
arranged by the Buyer.

## Section 5 — Documentation Requirements
Each shipment must include a commercial invoice, packing list, and,
for cross-border shipments, a certificate of origin. Missing
documentation that causes a customs delay is attributable to the
Supplier under Section 2.
